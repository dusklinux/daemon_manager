## Test method and limits

Individual optional-candidate tests temporarily boot out one registered job, sample six 30-second intervals, bootstrap it again and sample two recovery intervals. An independent child attempts bootstrap after four minutes if the test stops responding. No persistent enable/disable override is written by these tests and no reboot is requested. A reboot or external service-setting change invalidates the comparison; a timer cannot recover every possible OS failure. Tests are restricted to the reviewed progressd, ndoagent and nand.aspcarry candidates. Broad radio/USB/logger groups and known bootloop/CPU-loop dependencies are not subjected to an unattended disable/reboot sequence.

A short live unregister/register test does not establish safe persisted disabling across userspace/full boot, overnight background work, maintenance triggers, low storage, all feature clients or service combinations. A missing process footprint is not a measured net free-memory or battery gain. No additional default-Off target is justified solely by such a scoped pass.

The restarted progressd comparison completed: 30.07-second baseline, 180.44 seconds absent across six intervals, successful bootstrap (exit 0), verified registration and 60.14 seconds across two recovery intervals. Before/after override outputs were identical. All pressure flags were 1. Screen-on interaction affected the early samples; later locked Off samples measured backboardd around 0.45–0.50% of one core, assetsd 0–0.005%, and SpringBoard as low as 0.108%. Recovery remained quiet. No sustained one-core loop was observed in this window. progressd was registered but had no resident process in that baseline sample, so the test does not show a reclaimed process footprint or net RAM gain. Education/ClassKit functionality and persisted Off/reboot behavior were not exercised; default remains On.

nand.aspcarry was registered with zero launches and no running process in the post-reboot profile. Its boot/daily/maintenance triggers were not forced, and no memory saving or safe maintenance opt-out is claimed. Default remains On.

The ndoagent comparison also completed with the phone locked: 30.07-second baseline, 180.43 seconds absent and 60.15 seconds recovery. It used 0% CPU and 2.188 MiB physical footprint at baseline. Off intervals kept pressure Normal, assetsd at 0%, SpringBoard at 0.121–0.573% and backboardd at 0.472–0.506% of one core. Bootstrap returned 0, registration was verified, the recovered process used 0% CPU/about 2.204 MiB, and before/after override outputs matched. This is a short unregister/register pass; upgrade/language/pairing triggers and persisted Off/reboot compatibility remain untested. No net RAM or battery gain is established and its default remains On.
